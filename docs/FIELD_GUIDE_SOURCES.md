# Field Guide source notes (owner's real-life use cases, Nov 1, 2026)

Raw material from the owner, kept as written (lightly condensed) so reviewers can check every claim. The reviewed and corrected version is in `docs/CHAMPIONS.md`. Some claims below are wrong or off-rule; see the review notes there before using any of it in the game.

## A. Professional and work life
- **Presentations without notes.** Memory palace. Map each slide or talking point to a piece of furniture at home. Point one is financial growth, station one is the front door: a gold tree bursts through the doorway.
- **Networking and names (Foer's approach).** Link a striking feature to a vivid image of the name. "Baker" with thick eyebrows: loaves baking on his eyebrows. "Sarah" with bright glasses: she tears (tear-ah) the glasses in half.
- **PINs, passwords, client phone numbers (Dominic System).** Letters for numbers (1=A, 2=B, 3=C, 4=D). PIN 1423 becomes AD and BC. AD is Albus Dumbledore, BC is Bill Clinton: Dumbledore casts a spell on Clinton.

## B. Routine and household
- **Mental shopping lists.** Peg system or body palace, head to toe. Milk pours down your hair, eggs crack over your shoulders, spinach stuffed in jacket pockets.
- **Daily tasks and errands (journey method).** Use the morning commute. Coffee shop, turnstile, office elevator hold the errands. A leaking battery at the coffee shop means buy AA batteries.
- **Where you put keys or wallet (mindful exaggeration).** When you set keys on the counter, take half a second to picture the counter exploding in flames.

## C. Learning and personal development
- **Vocabulary and languages (link word).** French "champignon" (mushroom): a mushroom in a champion's one-piece swimsuit.
- **Books, articles, podcasts (Roman room).** One room per book. Each profound idea becomes a symbolic object placed in the room.

## D. Skill acquisition
1. **Coding.** Virtual palace from a video game map or museum. A `while` loop is a whirlpool in a hallway, with an `if` gatekeeper holding a python inside. Dominic System for syntax: `.append()` is an apple flung at a wall.
2. **Music.** Peg system and spatial mapping. Sharps order F C G D A E B: "Fat Cat God" sitting on the guitar headstock. Chord progression I V vi IV via number shapes (1 candle, 5 hook, 6 elephant trunk, 4 yacht sail).
3. **Physical skills and martial arts (BJJ, dance, golf).** First-person journey method. A five-step escape: bridge at the front porch, shrimp at the door handle, underhook at the coat rack. Walk the house mentally before class.
4. **Business and leadership.** Body palace for a negotiation framework: forehead interests, eyes options, mouth criteria. Touch your chin or temple mid-meeting to check the map.
- **Skill Accelerator method.** 1) Deconstruct: the 15 to 20 most critical rules, terms or movements (80/20). 2) Compress: turn each into a vivid, ridiculous 3D image. 3) Anchor: put the images in a dedicated "Skill Palace" used only for that hobby.

## E. Speed math
1. **Holding carries and intermediate results.** 43 x 67: 40x60 = 2400, 40x7 = 280, total 2680, but 3x67 = 201 is computed after you have forgotten 2680. Turn 2680 into letters (2=B, 6=F, 8=H, 0=O): BF and HO. Bob Fett hands a bounty to Santa ("Ho Oho") on your keyboard. Clear your mind, compute 201, glance at the keyboard, add: 2881.
2. **Constants and formulas.** Area of a circle A = pi r^2 as a story: an Apple sliced by a Pie while a Rabbit watches wearing a Square academic cap. Pi to 8 places (3.14159265) as pairs 14, 15, 92, 65 in the Dominic System, "a five-second movie".
3. **Everyday finance and percentages.** Tip 15% on $114.60 split three ways: 10% is 11.46 (peg to forehead), half of it 5.73 (peg to nose), sum 17.19, split by 3 is 5.73.
- **Setup.** Dominic System (digits to letters to person plus action; 3232 is Charlie Brown flying a kite, person for the first half, action for the second). Major System (digits to consonant sounds: 3=M, 2=N, so "M-N" is "mine" or "man").

## F. Remembering book lessons
1. **Author's studio (frameworks).** One room per book. Atomic Habits: a glowing neon sign at the door (obvious), a supermodel with chocolate on the couch (attractive), an Easy Button on the coffee table (easy), glitter and confetti explosion on the TV (satisfying). Teleport mentally to the room when facing a bad habit.
2. **Anecdote anchor (case studies).** Compress a case study to one bizarre image tied to the author's name: Gladwell on a crashing plane wing whispering into a megaphone to a pilot in a giant crown (mitigated speech, hierarchy).
3. **Actionable index (quotes and rules).** Body palace for rules of a book: Rule 1 "stand up straight" on the collarbone, Rule 2 on the chest, Rule 3 on the right hand.
- **Read to Remember workflow.** Margin marker (one gold nugget per chapter); chapter stop (close the book 30 seconds, make it a 3D image); bookmark palace (use the room you are in: window, lamp, rug); next-day review (glance at them before opening the book).
