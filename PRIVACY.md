# Privacy

Mnemos has no accounts, no analytics, no adverts and no trackers, and makes no network requests while you play. A unit test (`tests/beta.test.js`) fails if the source ever adds one.

- Everything you do is saved only in your browser (IndexedDB and localStorage).
- Backups are files you save yourself. They contain your alias, palace names and training history.
- The closed-beta report (Keeper > Closed beta) contains numbers only: no alias, no palace names, nothing you typed. You can read it before saving, and you send it yourself.
- Error messages are kept on your device (the last 20) and only leave it inside a report you choose to send.
- Deleting your data in Keeper removes it from this browser completely.
